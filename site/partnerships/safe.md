---
heading: "Why it's safe — a written, de-risked structure"
phases:
  - name: "Before anything is signed"
    items:
      - "<b>Clear written responsibilities</b> — and every contribution, including time, given a fair, recorded value."
      - "<b>Self-dealing agreed openly, or not at all.</b> Where a partner's own company is paid to provide services to the venture, the terms — at cost, discounted, or counted as contribution — are agreed up front, so no one earns ownership and separately profits from the venture's spending without everyone knowing."
      - "<b>Backing off is defined in advance</b> — what counts as not delivering, and how a partner exits, agreed before anyone needs it, so there are no bitter disputes later."
  - name: "At signing"
    items:
      - "<b>Funding secured up front, released against milestones (escrow).</b> We build against money that's actually secured, not a promise — deposited before serious building begins, spent gradually as stages prove out."
  - name: "While we build"
    items:
      - "<b>Staged and gated.</b> The next stage isn't funded until the last one shows real signs of working — the paid discovery phase is stage one of exactly this discipline."
      - "<b>Earned ownership over time (vesting).</b> Each partner's share builds as they deliver. Step away early and you keep only what you genuinely earned — which protects whoever stays."
      - "<b>Lean and pay-as-you-grow.</b> Modest tech costs; spend where it's the edge, keep the rest cheap."
  - name: "If someone steps back"
    items:
      - "<b>Work already done is honored.</b> The recorded value of completed work is settled first from the fund — the builder is not left having worked for nothing, and the same protection runs in every direction."
---

The riskiest part of a partnership isn't the build — it's what happens when money, ownership, and commitment meet uncertainty.

<p class="mt-pull">Good fences make <em>good partners</em>. The structure is in writing from the first conversation.</p>
