And we go deeper than most

We don't just use technology — we make it. Which is the rigor your project inherits.

Most software firms assemble tools. Over the years we've built and published our own — libraries, frameworks, even data and language standards. We don't make you adopt any of it; we build on mainstream technology. But it's why our engineering runs deep — and you can check all of it yourself.

Internet Object
A compact data format

Roughly 40% smaller than minified JSON, with ~30% fewer LLM tokens — and a live playground you can try against JSON yourself.

UExL
Our expression engine

Zero allocations on the boolean and string paths, and fastest in the scenarios we measured — reproduce the benchmark yourself.

Open source
Public Go libraries

Open-source Go libraries on our GitHub — including signals, our event library — open for anyone to read and run. Taj Mahal (this very site) and Processious are ours too.
