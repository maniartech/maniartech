heading: "Questions about how we work"
faqs:
  - q: How do you decide which engagement model fits my project?
    a: We look at how well-defined and how complex the work is. A large or evolving build gets a dedicated team, a tightly bounded job can be fixed scope, and open-ended work fits time and materials. If you are not sure, a senior engineer will talk it through and recommend the honest fit rather than the biggest one.
  - q: Can work start before the whole team is in place?
    a: Usually yes. For a dedicated-team engagement a senior engineer often begins straight away, and further engineers join as the project demands, so progress does not sit and wait on staffing.
  - q: How do you keep quality consistent across a project?
    a: Everything is reviewed before it reaches you, defects are fixed at the root so they stay fixed, and the whole process runs under our ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 certified system. As a rule most work clears the first client review with only minor changes.
  - q: How do I verify your ISO certifications?
    a: Email the registrar URS at info@urs-certification.com and quote the certificate number - 123961/B/0001 for quality or 123961/A/0001 for security. They will confirm the certificates directly, so you never have to take our word for it.
