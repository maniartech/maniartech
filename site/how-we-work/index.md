Choosing who builds your software is, more than anything, a bet on how a team behaves when you are not watching. This page is here so you do not have to guess. It lays out how we take on work, how a project moves from a first conversation to a running system, what we hold ourselves to along the way, and how the result is handed over to the people who will run it.

Every claim on this page is meant to be checkable - a certificate you can verify with the registrar, a way of working you can hold us to, a process written down rather than described in a meeting. That is the point of writing it down.
