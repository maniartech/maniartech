---
heading: "What ISO 27001 certification means in practice"
---

A certificate does not make software secure - engineering does. What certification proves is narrower, and more useful to a reviewer: our information-security management is a documented process, examined against the standard by an accredited external auditor, and re-examined on a recurring audit cycle. It is maintained, not awarded once and framed.

For a firm our size that is an uncommon commitment. Most companies at boutique scale never take it on, because holding a certificate means keeping the documentation, the discipline, and the audit schedule alive year after year. We took it on because process is how security stays predictable - captured in audited systems rather than in any one person's head.
