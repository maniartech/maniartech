---
heading: "Built to be handed over"
---

- **Mainstream, hireable technology on purpose.** We build with tools any competent team can pick up - commonly Go, Python, React, and PostgreSQL or MongoDB - so the people who maintain the system later need no private knowledge of ours.
- **Documentation is part of done.** Architecture notes, setup and deployment steps, and the decisions behind them are written as the work happens, not reconstructed at the end when everyone has forgotten why.
- **Technology decisions are written down.** Stack choices, dependencies, and architecture calls are documented and agreed with you while the project runs, so nothing in your system is a surprise later.
- **Confidentiality first is fine by us.** Want a signed NDA in place before we get into specifics? That is a normal way to start - send yours, or ask for ours.
