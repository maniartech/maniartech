> For an established Mumbai-region real-estate developer: a project showcase, a slide-based presentation builder, interactive floor plans, a full estimation-and-payment-plan engine, and a digital asset library — built end to end in a matter of months, going live in 2026.

## The situation

An established real-estate developer in the Mumbai metropolitan region came to us to run their entire presales the right way. They had a strong track record of delivered homes, but presales still happened the hard way — property demonstrations, cost estimates, inventory, and customer follow-up spread across people, slides, and spreadsheets.

They wanted one end-to-end platform their sales team could run the whole presales journey on: present projects beautifully on a tablet, walk a customer through floor plans, amenities and pricing, produce a professional, accurate cost estimate on the spot, and share it — all while keeping live inventory visible to sales staff only.

## What we built

A single platform that carries presales from first impression to a signed-off estimate:

- **An integrated showcase + presentation builder** — a polished, mobile- and tablet-ready presentation of the company and its projects, plus a slide-based "story" builder (themes, transitions, per-slide media) so sales teams can craft and publish guided, branded walkthroughs without touching a designer. All of it editable through an admin panel — no developer needed to change content.
- **A projects portfolio** — every project in one place, each with its units, wings, amenities, floor plans, brochures and estimations, tagged residential, commercial or featured.
- **Interactive floor plans** — unit selection and zoom across 1/2/3BHK layouts, alongside amenities, location maps with connectivity, transparent price lists, and downloadable brochures.
- **An estimation engine** — the hard part. It computes a full, real-estate-grade cost (agreement value, GST, registration, stamp duty, development and legal charges, amenities and maintenance), lays out a milestone-by-milestone payment plan tied to construction-completion stages, applies a governed discount-approval workflow, and produces a clean, branded, RERA-referenced estimate to print or share over WhatsApp and email.
- **A digital asset library** — a built-in manager for project images, brochures, floor plans and video, with previews.
- **Masters + access control** — configurable amenities and tax-jurisdiction masters, with role-based users and groups, including sales-only inventory (available / on hold / sold) that staff see and customers never do.

The whole platform — showcase, presentation builder, portfolio, floor plans, estimation engine, asset library, masters and access control — was built in roughly **three to four months**, on a current stack: Django 6, React 19, PostgreSQL, and AWS.
