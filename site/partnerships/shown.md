heading: "How we work — shown, not claimed"
---

A plan you can trust beats a plan that sounds confident. The single thing a co-founder owes you is a straight read of the venture — before you commit, not after. We won't tell you we're "the honest ones"; that's the exact line a sales pitch uses. Instead, here is what the working documents look like — the format, shown openly; we'll walk you through redacted real examples in conversation:

<pre class="sd-body">Build phase ....  6-8 weeks
                  <span class="t-est">[ESTIMATE - a range,
                   not a promise]</span>
Gateway support   confirmed
                  <span class="t-fact">[FACT - verified]</span>

<div class="sd-cap">Estimates marked as estimates, facts as facts — and what can't be confirmed says so, instead of being dressed up as certainty.</div>
