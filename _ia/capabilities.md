## 3 · WHEN THERE'S NO EXACT MATCH  *(the honest answer for genuinely new work)*

Sometimes part of what you need doesn't have a lookalike anywhere — and honestly, if a vendor shows you
a perfect match for something truly specific, they're usually selling you a recycled template.

That's the kind of work we've done for fifteen years: **production NLP in 2010, before modern AI**
([Content Engine](/case-studies/content-engine/)); a **pioneering customer-journey platform in 2012**
([Touchpoint](/case-studies/touchpoint/)); **drawing live on running video in the browser in 2019**
([the video studio](/case-studies/upsport/)). Each had no twin when we built it.

So when the work is new, we don't promise and hope — **we de-risk it with a paid proof-of-concept:** a
small, time-boxed build that proves the hard part works *before* you commit to the full project. You see
it running first. (Both the platforms above started exactly that way.)
