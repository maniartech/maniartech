---
heading: "How deep our AI engineering goes"
---

Plenty of teams can wire up a chatbot. Fewer can engineer an AI system solid enough to put their own name on it. Documentor is our example of that depth - a document platform built from several parts working together: the engine that generates and revises, the store that holds your uploaded knowledge, the editor you write in, and the orchestration that ties them. Under the hood it carries out a goal step by step, grounds its answers in your own documents rather than making them up, picks a suitable model for each task, and keeps a person in the loop by design.

We do not make you adopt any of it - your project is built on mainstream tools you can maintain. But it is why we can take on AI work that goes past the obvious, and engineer it carefully. It is the same discipline behind the platforms and tooling we build for ourselves.

- [Documentor](/products/documentor/), the AI document platform we are building - early-stage and in development, shown as proof of how we engineer AI, not as a service for sale today.
