---
heading: "How a project moves, and what you get at each step"
stages:
  - name: "Discovery"
    what: "We learn the problem before we price it - who uses the system, what breaks in the current way of doing things, and what already exists that must be kept. Nothing is proposed until we understand what we are proposing."
    gives: "A written scope and a ballpark you are free to push back on."
  - name: "Architecture"
    what: "The shape of the system is decided in the open - technology choices, the data model, the integrations - and the reasoning is written in plain language, not buried in code."
    gives: "An architecture you can read, own, and question."
  - name: "Build"
    what: "Experienced engineers build in small, reviewed steps. You watch working software take shape rather than waiting for one large reveal at the end that lands wrong."
    gives: "Working software in increments, every one checked over before you see it."
  - name: "Ship"
    what: "We deploy to production, document what was built, and hand it over under our audited process - so the system does not live in one person's head."
    gives: "A running, documented system your own team can operate."
  - name: "Support"
    what: "We stay available for fixes and changes, and we repair causes rather than symptoms, so the same problem does not return wearing a different mask."
    gives: "A system that keeps working - and does not need us to stay alive."
---
