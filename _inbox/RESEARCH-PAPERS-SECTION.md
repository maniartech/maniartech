## What it is (and what it's NOT)
- **IS:** formal, substantive papers/studies/whitepapers on applied topics relevant to
  clients' businesses — behavioral/UX, enterprise strategy, process design, AI adoption,
  human factors in software, etc. Web page + downloadable PDF. Authored (founder/team byline).
- **NOT the Labs.** Labs = software/standards/tools we BUILD. Papers = studies we WRITE.
  Both ladder up to "ManiarTech is a software engineering & **research** company."
- **More substantial than blog posts.** Blog/Insights = timely, lighter. Papers = evidence-
  based, a point of view, designed to be kept and shared.

## Why it's a strong move (4 strategic payoffs)
1. **Elevates vendor → strategic partner.** A buyer who reads a sharp paper on
   gamification-in-enterprise thinks *"these people understand my business, not just code."*
   That reframes the relationship from "dev shop we hire" to "advisors we trust" — and
   justifies premium positioning (counterweights the ~50%-cost message: cheaper AND smarter).
2. **Closes the offshore TRUST GAP** — same mechanism as the estimator and case studies:
   **competence shown beats competence claimed.** Senior strategic thinking on the page is
   high-credibility proof, exactly what skeptical US/EU buyers need.
3. **Distribution + SEO engine** — papers are highly shareable (LinkedIn!), rank for
   high-intent business searches, and work as **lead magnets**. Directly serves the
   distribution problem (DISTRIBUTION-STRATEGY.md). A paper = a durable, compounding asset.
4. **Sales enablement** — "shared with clients" = conversation-starters and credibility tools
   in the sales process; partners can use them too (like the estimator).

## ⚠️ The make-or-break: QUALITY BAR (same as case studies)
Thought-leadership is a double-edged sword. **Shallow, SEO-baity, or AI-slop papers HURT a
trust brand** (DISTRIBUTION-STRATEGY warns: low-quality AI content is penalized by search and
resented by readers). For ManiarTech specifically, this MUST clear a high bar:
- **Original point of view**, not a regurgitated listicle.
- **Evidence-based** — cite data, research, real examples; honest about uncertainty.
- **Actionable** for the client's actual decisions.
- **Genuinely insightful** — the reader learns something they didn't know.
- **On-brand honesty** — no overclaiming; same ethos as the engineering.
→ **A few genuinely excellent papers >> many shallow ones.** Quality over quantity, always.
Start with 2–3 strong ones, not a content farm.
