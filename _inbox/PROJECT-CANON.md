## 0. The two rules that govern the whole project

1. **⛔ GOVERNING RULE #1 — NOWHERE exaggerate or overpromise. ZERO.** Aamir's own
   standard, emphatic and repeated ("we'll never go to market with fabricated things").
   Every line passes a **Claim Audit**: *Is it true? Is it sourced? Is it calibrated? Does
   the status label match reality?* Banned: "world-class/best/leading/fastest" without
   proof, invented numbers, fake scale/clients/stars, non-reproducible benchmarks,
   "production-ready" for beta. Allowed: bold framing of *real* substance, "we can build X"
   when the Labs prove the ceiling, honest ranges, reproducible benchmarks with method
   shown. **This is the moat** — one caught exaggeration discredits everything for an
   offshore-trust-gap brand. The truth is impressive enough; show it, never inflate it.

2. **★ PRESENTATION DOCTRINE — the breakthrough; the spine above even Rule #1.**
   *Lead with their safety, not your brilliance. Same substance, opposite order.* The
   diagnosis (Aamir confirmed: "this is the reason"): low client results were never a
   substance problem — they were a **presentation** problem. Leading with "we make
   technology / we author standards / depth" reads to a *skeptical buyer* as
   risky/unproven/one-person/lock-in/not-for-me — even though it's magnetic to
   *engineers/recruits*. The two audiences need opposite things in the first 30 seconds.
   - **Presentation order for ALL client material** (site, decks, proposals, LinkedIn,
     pitches): **their problem → proof you deliver → why it's safe → JP Morgan credibility
     → depth (the closer) → low-risk next step.**
   - **Audience exception:** depth-first IS correct for recruiting / developer / OSS / peer
     audiences (Careers page, Labs page).
   - Canonical doc: `PRESENTATION-DOCTRINE.md`. Paired test: the **Customer-Lens / Skeptic
     Test** (`customer-lens-review.md`) — review every page as a skeptical stranger, not a
     proud builder.

## 4. Services (FINAL: 3 categories + 2 cross-cutting capabilities)

Earlier attempts (7 flat services, then 5, then a 4-category "Enterprise SE / Application
SE" pair) **overlapped** — Aamir flagged it ("Enterprise may include AI; Modernization may
be modernization of Enterprise software"). Root cause I diagnosed: software services have
**three orthogonal axes** — Domain (operations vs applications), Lifecycle (build vs
modernize), Technology (AI, Go). **AI and Modernization are *capabilities*, not domains** —
forcing them to be peer categories created the bleed. Final structure:

**Categories — the "what" (non-overlapping):**
1. **⭐ Enterprise Software Engineering** — "systems that run your operations." Contains
   **Business Process Automation & Integration** (flagship, the *ManiarTech Keystone
   Method*) + **Enterprise DAM** (on the Tallery framework) + **Business Analytics**
   (capability, no track-record claim). Proof: Processious, Ordin, Engage.
2. **Application Software Engineering** — "applications & products built for you." Custom
   application development + architecture review & advisory (two pillars: **Build** +
   **Advise**).
3. **Technology Partnership** — ManiarTech as **technology co-founder** (valued time →
   earned equity, shared risk; "build *with* you, not *for* you"). ⚠️ **No completed
   venture yet → selective/emerging, never claim a track record.** Distinct from the
   *delivery/channel* partnership growth model (§10).

**Cross-cutting capabilities — the "how" (woven through 1 & 2, each with its own showcase
page, NOT peer categories):**
- **AI** — agentic / RAG / knowledge-grounded, across operations + applications. Proof:
  **Documentor** + the AI Estimator.
- **Modernization & Migration** — existing systems → modern stacks (incl. Go). Proof: our
  **own rebuilds** + the **authored Go ecosystem + Indigo**. Honest polyglot framing.

Homepage shows 3 category cards + a line: *"Brought to every engagement — AI and
Modernization & Migration."*

### ⭐ The ManiarTech Keystone Method (the flagship method)
- **Consultative / understand-first** value-ladder service; the commercial face of the
  whole portfolio. **Method = department/module-wise:** every department has its own
  roles/mission/vision (e.g. HR isn't just recruitment — it's keeping staff **motivated +
  skilled**, because a sharp motivated team reaches the vision).
- **Targets three enterprise "quiet killers": supervision cost · human bias (favoritism) ·
  single-point dependency / bus-factor** — all solved by **documenting EVERYTHING**
  (processes, formats, procedures, records) so knowledge lives in the *system*, not in
  individuals. Replicable across sales/manufacturing/accounts/marketing/etc.
- **★ Name DECIDED: "The ManiarTech Keystone Method."** Always the **ManiarTech Keystone
  lockup**, NEVER bare "Keystone" (collides with Keystone Strategy / Keystone AI + a live
  USPTO filing; the "Keystone Method" construction itself is unclaimed). Arch metaphor: each
  department = a stone; the documented system = the **keystone** that locks the arch so it's

  (a monument of arches) brand. Killer line: *"We don't prop your business up with
  consultants — we set the keystone so it stands on its own."*
- **5 formal stages** (each has a deliverable + a value-ladder rung): **Survey** (understand
  the department) · **Document** (capture processes/records) · **Design** (recommend +
  design systems, incl. where it *won't* help) · **Set the Keystone** (build + integrate,
  self-supporting) · **Bear Load** (uplift motivation/skill, measure, iterate → next
  department). Proof wiring: Processious builds Stage 4, Engage = Stage 5, Documentor =
  Stage 2.
- **★ Two metaphors, kept separate:** *"It's not a palace in the sky — we know the stairs to
  reach it"* = the emotional **pitch line** (the value ladder: processes → results →
  satisfaction → motivation → numbers → vision/mission). **Keystone** = the named **method**
  (the arch). Never blend them in one paragraph.
- **Honesty guardrail:** the method promises a *path*, not guaranteed ROI — frame as
