# One platform to run a developer's entire presales — from showcase to signed estimate

> A complete Sales Navigator for an established Mumbai-region real-estate developer: a project showcase, a
> slide-based presentation builder, a digital asset library, interactive floor plans, and a full
> estimation-and-payment-plan engine — built end to end in a matter of months, launching 2026.

> **Client:** an established residential & commercial developer in the Mumbai metropolitan region
> (2000+ families served) · **Work:** a full presales platform, built end to end · **Status:** in final
> development, launching 2026 · **Scope:** project showcase · property detail + floor plans · estimation
> engine (taxes, charges, discounts) · sales-only inventory · WhatsApp/Email sharing.

## The challenge
An established developer with thousands of homes delivered still ran presales the hard way — property
demonstrations, cost estimates, inventory, and customer follow-up spread across people, slides, and
spreadsheets. They wanted one platform their sales team could run the whole presales journey on:
present projects beautifully on a tablet, walk a customer through floor plans, amenities and pricing,
generate a professional, accurate cost estimate on the spot, and share it — all while keeping live
inventory visible to sales staff only.

## What we're building
A single platform that carries presales from first impression to a signed-off estimate:

- **An integrated showcase** — a polished, mobile- and tablet-ready presentation of the company and its
  projects, fully editable through an admin panel (no developer needed to update content).
- **Property detail pages** — interactive floor plans with unit selection and zoom, amenities, location
  maps with connectivity, transparent price lists, and downloadable brochures.
- **An estimation engine** — the hard part. It computes a full, real-estate-grade cost — agreement value,
  GST, registration, stamp duty, development and legal charges, amenities and maintenance — lays out a
  milestone-by-milestone payment plan tied to construction stages, applies a governed discount-approval
  workflow, and produces a clean, branded, RERA-referenced estimate to print or share over WhatsApp and email.
- **A slide-based presentation builder** — so sales teams can craft and publish guided, branded project
  "stories" to walk a customer through, without touching a designer.
- **A project portfolio + digital asset library** — every project, its units, plans and amenities in one
  place, backed by a built-in library for images, brochures, floor plans and video.
- **Sales-only inventory** — unit availability (available / on hold / sold) that sales staff see and
  customers never do.

We scoped it deliberately to **presales** — not bookings, payments, or post-sales — with a foundation
built to extend into full sales management later. That understand-first, build-in-stages discipline is
how we keep a system genuinely fit for the business rather than a sprawl of half-used features.
