## The spine: one idea, shown five ways

"We don't prop your business up with consultants - we set the keystone so it stands on its own."
(Canon, Keystone Method.) A documented system, not a person, holds the load. Everything
below visualises that idea or the proof behind it, so the page reads as one argument.

## What I need from you (only these)

1. **Vision and mission wording.** The canon has the ambition ("sky-rocket", "people take a
   look and say: this is the one") but not a published vision or mission. Shall I draft both
   from the canon, in your understated voice, for you to edit?
2. **Objectives.** What should a visitor understand ManiarTech is aiming at? Three lines from
   you is enough.
3. **Stage names.** The canon leaves open whether "Set the Keystone" and "Bear Load" ship as
   written, or soften to Survey, Document, Design, Build, Sustain. My recommendation: keep the
   vivid names, with plain-English subtitles.

## Guardrails I would keep

- Every scene is labelled "illustration" and uses no invented numbers.
- The Keystone Method promises a path, not guaranteed ROI ("systems that aim to...").
- Real content at rest, reduced-motion shows a finished frame, phone layout stacks, no hover
  on moving shapes, dark and mint only.
- Private projects stay labelled private; Tallery, Growthicious and Ordin stay off.

## Build order I would suggest

1. Process: five stages (the centrepiece), then 2. quiet killers, then 3. vision arch,
   then 4. Foundry graph, then 5. how a change travels. Each reviewed with you before the next.
