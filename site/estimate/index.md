Describe your project in your own words. A senior engineer reads it and replies with a **ballpark estimate** plus an honest first read on the shape of the work. No sales call to sit through. No obligation. Just a fast, straight answer from people who've been shipping software for clients since 2010.

## You'll know quickly whether we understand a problem like yours

You're not buying a number. You're checking, with low effort and zero commitment, whether the people you'd hand this to actually grasp what you're trying to build. So the read you get back names the **decisions that genuinely change the shape of the work** - the trade-offs a senior engineer would flag - not a generic price pulled from a form.

If we've understood it, you'll feel it in the response. It shows its assumptions, so you can see exactly what we took the estimate to mean. And if it's not a fit, you'll know that fast too - we'll say so.
