## Why AI with us is safe

AI raises real worries. Here's how we handle each one:

- **A human stays in the loop.** We use AI to assist people, not to replace their judgment. The AI does the heavy lifting and asks a person when it's unsure. We don't ship decisions no one checked.
- **It works from your facts, not made-up ones.** Where AI answers questions, we ground it in *your* documents and data so it cites real sources instead of confidently inventing things.
- **Your data stays yours.** Your documents, data, and prompts are not used to train any third-party model. We're explicit, up front, about what the AI can see and where that data goes.
- **No black box.** You'll understand what the AI is doing, where it's used, and where it deliberately isn't. If a plain, non-AI solution is safer or cheaper, we'll tell you that.
- **No lock-in.** We build on mainstream technology your own team — or any team — can hire for. We don't trap you in AI tooling only we understand.
- **A disciplined, certified security process.** Your data sits inside an ISO/IEC 27001:2022-certified information-security process (and ISO 9001:2015 for quality) — certified by URS under UKAS accreditation (cert. 123961/A/0001 and 123961/B/0001; verify at info@urs-certification.com).
