# Site review, October 2026

Reference notes from the first review of the local site (Home and About), kept so the reasoning is
not lost.

- `home-and-positioning-review.md`: is the site too consultancy-oriented, what a product and
  engineering-led position looks like, which Home and About visuals work and why, a comparison
  with coderabbit.ai, and a prioritised change list.
- `about-review.md`: About page, section by section, with claim and canon issues.

## Headline findings

- Home reads as consultancy because the copy is made of adjectives and the specific, impressive
  work sits late. That follows `_inbox/PRESENTATION-DOCTRINE.md` (depth as the closer), so changing
  it is a strategy decision. The agreed route (Aamir, 2026-10-20): keep the hero, make everything
  below it specific and visual.
- The strongest visual on the site was the real Processious screenshot. Decoration (marquee,
  abstract service-card art) added motion without information.
- CodeRabbit's strengths worth copying: a thesis headline, product shown as realistic UI, proof
  directly after the hero, motion only inside the product panels, one primary call to action.
