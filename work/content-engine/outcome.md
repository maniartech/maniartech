## How it turned out

Content Engine was delivered in three months and ran in production for about two years inside Euclid Infotech's editorial pipeline, giving the copywriting team grammar-safe, sense-aware rewriting suggestions instead of rewriting every line from scratch. We have no throughput figures from that era, and we will not invent any; the honest record is the run itself - two years of daily use in a real newsroom-style workflow.

A note on the testimonial we carry on this page: Euclid Infotech (TendersInfo.com) was a multi-project client, and Content Engine was one of several systems we built with them. Shailesh Pichori's words speak to that broader relationship - the web portal and Windows application work - rather than being a review of Content Engine specifically. We quote it for exactly what it is: a client who worked with us across multiple projects and was glad they did.

The longer outcome is lineage. Word-sense disambiguation, human-in-the-loop editorial assist, governed sign-off - in 2010, on classical NLP, we were building for the appetite generative AI would serve a decade later. That work is part of the depth behind the AI engineering we do now.
