## 4 · WHY AI WITH US IS SAFE  *(de-risk — answers the specific fears AI raises)*

### AI raises real worries. Here's how we handle each one.

- **A human stays in the loop.** We use AI to assist and accelerate people, not to replace their
  judgment. Our own products are built this way — the AI does the heavy lifting and asks a person
  when it's unsure. We don't ship decisions no one checked.
- **It works from your facts, not made-up ones.** Where AI answers questions, we ground it in
  *your* documents and data so it cites real sources instead of confidently inventing things —
  the same approach behind Documentor.
- **Your data stays yours.** Your documents, data, and prompts are not used to train any
  third-party model. We're explicit, up front, about what the AI can see, where that data goes, and
  who processes it — no surprises, no quiet data leakage into a vendor's model.
- **No black box.** You'll understand what the AI is doing, where it's used, and where it
  deliberately isn't. If a plain, non-AI solution is safer or cheaper, we'll tell you that.
- **No lock-in.** We build on mainstream technology your own team — or any team — can hire for and
  maintain. We don't trap you in AI tooling only we understand. `[mainstream-stack reassurance,
  consistent with home §4 / about §3. Keep the wording stack-honest; avoid naming specific model
  vendors as a permanent commitment.]`
- **Right-sized, not AI for its own sake.** We add AI where it earns its place. We won't turn a
  simple need into an expensive science experiment to look impressive.
- **Handled with a disciplined, certified security process.** Your data sits inside an **ISO/IEC
  27001:2022**-certified information-security process (and ISO 9001:2015 for quality) — certified by
  **URS** under **UKAS** accreditation (cert. 123961/A/0001 & 123961/B/0001; verify at
