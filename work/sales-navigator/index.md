## The situation

An established real-estate developer in the Mumbai metropolitan region - more than two thousand homes delivered since the late 1990s - still ran presales the hard way. Property demonstrations, cost estimates, inventory, and customer follow-up lived across people, slides, and spreadsheets. Every sales conversation depended on who was in the room and which version of which file they had.

Anyone who has bought a flat knows the moment that matters: sitting across from a salesperson, trying to understand what a unit actually costs. Agreement value, GST, stamp duty, registration, development charges, amenities, a payment plan tied to construction milestones - assembled by hand, differently by each salesperson, it is slow and easy to get wrong. And a wrong number in a property estimate is not a typo; it is a trust problem with a customer making the largest purchase of their life.

The developer wanted one platform their sales team could run the whole presales journey on: present projects properly on a tablet, walk a customer through floor plans, amenities and pricing, produce an accurate, professional cost estimate on the spot, and share it over WhatsApp or email - all while keeping live unit inventory visible to sales staff only, never to customers.
