Across real operations, the AI that earns its keep does one of two jobs: **answering questions from your own documents** so people stop hunting through folders, or **carrying the repetitive reading and typing** so people spend their day on judgment calls. Everything below is those two jobs, with the mechanics stated - and we are candid about which one your problem needs, or whether it needs AI at all.
