---
heading: "Modernizing with us does not trade one risk for another"
---

- **No lock-in.** The whole point of modernizing is to get off something only a few people can still maintain - so we will not put you back there. You land on mainstream, hireable technology, commonly Go, Python, React, and PostgreSQL, that your own team or any team can run.
- **Experienced engineers, and not just one.** Migrations are where the buried risks live, so experienced people do the work and we do not learn on your system. The plan and the work are shared across the team with a clear handover, so the project does not stall if one person is away.
- **Right-sized, not over-engineered.** We modernize to solve your actual problem at the right scale. We will not turn a working system into a science experiment.
- **Controlled by process.** We work to documented quality and security processes - ISO 9001:2015 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified by URS under UKAS accreditation, cert. 123961/B/0001 and 123961/A/0001, verifiable with the registrar. On a migration that means change is reviewed and auditable, not ad hoc.
