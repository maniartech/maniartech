## 4 · HOW IT WORKS  *(set expectations — low commitment, status-honest)*

### A short intake. A senior read. No obligation.

1. **Tell us what you're building.** A custom application, an automation, a system that needs
   modernizing, an AI feature — in your own words. A few details about scope and constraints help.
2. **A senior engineer reviews it.** Not a bot, not a junior — someone who actually scopes work like
   this `[interim behavior; see build-note]`.
3. **You get a ballpark estimate.** A realistic range for what the work involves — and a clear next
   step if you'd like to take it further.

> **It's an estimate, not a final quote.** A real quote comes after we understand your project
> properly — with a person, in a real conversation. This is the fast, no-pressure first look.
