Open source

Not a category — a habit.

Most of what we build, we open. That's why "open source" isn't a separate shelf above — it's a tag on the things that carry it. Every public repository lives on our GitHub: read it, run it, or reproduce a benchmark yourself. The point isn't to be admired for it; it's that you don't have to take our word for the engineering — you can check it.
