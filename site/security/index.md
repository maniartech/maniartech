Working with an outside engineering firm means handing over things you cannot afford to lose: source code, business data, credentials, sometimes regulated records. If you are the person who has to sign off on that - procurement, a security reviewer, or a founder doing both jobs - this page is for you. It is deliberately short: a few claims, each one checkable.
