What you are seeing

Lit, shadowed stone with real bevels. Dust that rises where each stone lands. A glow that blooms when the keystone locks. A lens that breathes and shakes on impact.

What it still is not

Hand-sculpted assets, a composer and a sound designer, and weeks of grading. Those are what a film studio adds. This is the ceiling of what runs in a browser without them.

On the site

It would load only when scrolled into view, fall back to a still frame without WebGL or on reduced motion, and cost roughly one image-heavy page of data.
