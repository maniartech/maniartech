## How it turned out

The engineering did what it set out to do. The proof-of-concept established that a 2019 browser could carry live video capture with live annotation; the full studio was then built and delivered - a coach recording commentary and drawing on running video, all captured in the browser, on WebRTC and our own WebDoodling framework. It remains one of the clearest examples of how we handle frontier work: de-risk first, then build.

We will not dress the rest up in metrics - an early-stage venture's numbers are its founder's story to tell, not ours. What is ours to show is what the founder said. Jeff Hines had already worked with us for years on Touchpoint Dashboard before bringing UpSport to us, and his words on this page - on time, high quality, fairly priced, excellent communicators - were written about this project's lifecycle. A founder who returns for his second venture, and then says that, is the outcome we would want a prospective client to weigh.
