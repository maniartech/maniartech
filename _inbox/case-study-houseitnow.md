## ✅ The PUBLISHABLE principle (abstract it — NO client, NO names, NO dispute)
The engineering *lesson* is gold and fully usable, **abstracted**:

> **We build protection in, even when it isn't asked for.** On one system, we added full **audit
> logging** — a record of who changed what, and when — that wasn't in the requirements. Later, that
> record answered a question no one else could: exactly what had changed, and by whom. Foresight like
> that never shows up in a demo; it shows up the day you need it.

- Use in **values-how-we-work** / "how we work" as a concrete *behaviour* (lived values, not a poster).
- Ties to the **data-integrity / audit** theme (Chemo's audit trail + secure dispatch; the ISO 27001
  security story).
- ★ KEEP IT ABSTRACT — no scandal, no deletion/denial specifics, no client, no people. Just: "we built
  an audit trail no one asked for; it later proved decisive."
