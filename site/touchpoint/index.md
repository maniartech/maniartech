A pioneering customer-journey management and analytics platform — where our founder spent six years as a principal architect, and which is documented in O'Reilly's *Mapping Experiences.*

## The situation

Touchpoint Dashboard set out to do something that, in 2012, very few products did well: let a brand map and visualise its customers' journeys, manage every touchpoint along the way, and bring stakeholders together around them. It was built for organisations serious about putting the customer first.

A platform like that needs real engineering depth. Journey-mapping, analytics, and multi-stakeholder collaboration are not trivial features to get right — and the product was aiming for an audience that would hold it to a high standard.

## What we built

This is the engagement that took ManiarTech international, and it's where our founder, **Aamir Maniar**, spent six years.

He joined the product's engineering team in 2012 — ManiarTech's first international project — coming on via UpWork as part of the product's team. He became one of its **principal architects**, designing and building many of the platform's core modules: the journey-mapping experience, the analytics, the collaboration features, and the continuous work of making it feel better to use. By his own account, he was responsible for the majority of the system's engineering. He joined an existing team — an earlier engineer had stood up the original infrastructure — and made it his own from there.

When Touchpoint Dashboard was **acquired by Strativity Inc.**, the relationship deepened. ManiarTech became its **offshore engineering partner** — a small dedicated team, led by Aamir, that carried the platform's engineering through to 2018. Six years, one product.
