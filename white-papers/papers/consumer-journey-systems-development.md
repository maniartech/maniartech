This paper is in preparation — here's what it will cover.

## Abstract

Custom systems are too often designed around internal data flows rather than the people who move through them. This paper looks at systems development through the lens of the consumer journey — the end-to-end experience of the people a system actually serves — and why that lens changes both what you build and the order you build it in.

## What it will cover

- What a consumer journey is in a systems context, and why it's easy to skip.
- Where journey thinking reshapes scope, priorities, and sequencing.
- Signals that a build is drifting away from the people it's meant to serve.
- Practical ways to keep the journey central without slowing delivery.
