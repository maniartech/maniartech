---
heading: "The shapes modernization work takes"
items:
  - name: "The audit & migration plan"
    line: "Before anything moves: an inventory of what the system actually does, its dependencies and risks, and a written plan you can challenge - including what is not worth migrating."
  - name: "Strangler-fig module migration"
    line: "The system replaced piece by piece while it keeps serving - old and new side by side, traffic moved gradually, every step reversible. No go-live weekend."
  - name: "Stack & language migration"
    line: "Onto Go where concurrency, throughput or compute cost genuinely benefit; onto current Python where that fits better. The workload chooses, not a house preference."
  - name: "Data migration & reconciliation"
    line: "Legacy data mapped, cleaned and moved - then PROVEN across, with counts and comparisons, because 'it looks right' is not a migration report."
  - name: "Performance re-engineering"
    line: "The one part buckling under load - rebuilt on an engine that fits, measured before and after, without dragging the rest of the system through a rewrite."
  - name: "The leave-it-alone verdict"
    line: "Some parts are ugly, stable and rarely touched - moving them is risk with no return. We put that in writing when it is true, and it often is."
---
