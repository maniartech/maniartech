---
heading: "Analytics & dashboards - numbers derived, never typed"
---

Enterprise analytics fails in one of two ways: a dashboard bolted onto data nobody trusts, or reports that take a person a day to assemble every month. Both have the same root - the numbers are not connected to the system doing the work.

Because our analytics rides on the workflow layer above, the metrics are *derived from states the system enforces*: turnaround time per stage, work pending by role, exception and rework rates, aging of everything unresolved, volumes by customer, by department, by month. Nobody re-types a number into a deck, which means nobody can mistype one either - the report and the operation cannot disagree, because they are the same data. We build the views three audiences actually need: the operator's live queue ("what needs me now"), the manager's flow view ("where is work piling up"), and the owner's trend view ("is this quarter better than last") - plus exports, because your accountant will want the raw sheet, and fighting that is a losing battle.
