Most AI pitches start with the technology; we start with the work. Across real operations, AI that earns its keep does one of two jobs - **answering questions from your own documents**, or **carrying the repetitive reading and typing** - and every card below is one of those two jobs wearing work clothes. The two mechanisms behind them are opened up in full underneath, safeguards included.
