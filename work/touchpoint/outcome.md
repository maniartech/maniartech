## How it turned out

The engagement ran six years - 2012 to 2018 - across one product, surviving the strongest test a vendor relationship faces: an acquisition. Strativity Group acquired Touchpoint Dashboard in 2015, and the relationship deepened rather than ended: from April 2017, ManiarTech served as the product's offshore engineering development partner, through July 2018.

The product's own standing is checkable. Touchpoint Dashboard is documented in O'Reilly's *Mapping Experiences: A Complete Guide to Creating Value through Journeys, Blueprints & Diagrams* by James Kalbach (Figure 6-21) - a published, independent reference anyone can look up. Many Fortune 500 teams used it. We understand it was, at one point, included in a University of Sydney marketing-management course. Those accolades belong to the product; our part was helping engineer it, and we state it exactly that way.

The clearest outcome is in the client's own words - the testimonial from Peter Haid, Strativity Group's Chief Product Officer, on this page: a named US executive describing ManiarTech as a loyal, trustworthy partner with a track record of transparency about both time and issues. For a buyer weighing an unfamiliar offshore team, that is the kind of proof we would want to see too.
