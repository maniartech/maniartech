## Abstract

Building a system is only half the work; getting people to actually use it is the other half. This paper examines how well-chosen game mechanics can raise engagement and adoption inside an organization — and, just as importantly, where they backfire.

## What it will cover

- What "motivation" really means at work, beyond points and badges.
- Where game mechanics help adoption — and where they erode trust or feel gimmicky.
- How this thinking informs Engage, and how to apply it to systems you already run.
- Honest limits: what game mechanics can't fix.
