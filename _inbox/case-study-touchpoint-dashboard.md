# Architecting a customer-journey platform that Fortune 500 brands relied on

> Touchpoint Dashboard — a pioneering customer-journey-mapping and analytics platform — was where our
> founder spent six years as a principal architect and developer. It's used by Fortune 500 brands and
> documented in O'Reilly's *Mapping Experiences.*

> **Product:** Touchpoint Dashboard, customer-journey management & analytics (later acquired by
> Strativity Inc.) · **Our role:** ManiarTech's founder, Aamir Maniar — principal architect &
> developer, then offshore team lead · **When:** 2012–2018 · **Recognition:** Fortune 500 users ·
> featured in O'Reilly's *Mapping Experiences* (James Kalbach) · used in a University of Sydney
> marketing course.

## What it is
Touchpoint Dashboard is a pioneer in **customer-journey management** — software that lets a brand map
and visualise its customers' journeys, manage every touchpoint, and collaborate with stakeholders
around them. It was built for organisations serious about putting the customer first, and it counted
**Fortune 500 brands** among its users.

## Our role
This is where ManiarTech's founder, **Aamir Maniar**, spent six years — and it's the engagement that
took ManiarTech international.

He joined the product's engineering team in 2012 and became one of its **principal architects and
developers**: designing and building many of the platform's core modules — the journey-mapping
experience, the analytics, the collaboration features — and continuously improving how it felt to use.
By his own account, he was responsible for the majority of the system's engineering. *(He joined an
existing team — an earlier engineer had stood up the original infrastructure — and made it his own
from there.)*

When Touchpoint Dashboard was **acquired by Strativity Inc.**, the relationship deepened: ManiarTech
became its **offshore development partner.** We built a small dedicated team, with Aamir leading it, and
carried the platform's engineering through to 2018 — a **six-year** run with a single product.

## Why this one matters
Three things a skeptic can check or weigh:

- **It was prestigious enough to be written about.** Touchpoint Dashboard is documented in O'Reilly's
  *Mapping Experiences: A Complete Guide to Creating Value through Journeys, Blueprints & Diagrams* by
  James Kalbach (Figure 6-21). That's a published, independent reference you can look up.
- **It was prestigious enough to be taught.** We understand the platform was, at one point, part of a
  **University of Sydney** marketing-management course.
- **It was trusted at scale, internationally, for years.** Fortune 500 brands used it; a US product
  company kept ManiarTech engaged on its core engineering for six years. For a buyer weighing an
  unfamiliar team, "they architected a platform serious enough to be taught and published" is a
  different order of proof than a logo wall.

And it's not a detour from what we do — customer-journey thinking (understand the whole journey, every
touchpoint, the people around it) is the same instinct behind how we approach business systems today.
