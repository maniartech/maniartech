ManiarTech was founded in 2010 by [Aamir Maniar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aamironline). Earlier
in his career he built financial-technology systems at **JP Morgan** — including a FIX-protocol
analyzer with its own script engine and compiler — and worked as a technology architect at firms
including **Countrywide Financial**, **Patni** (now iGATE), and **Oakton**.

Across 27 years, he has specialized in the part most engineers avoid: the architecture and the
foundational systems that everything else depends on.
