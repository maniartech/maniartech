Most work worth doing is complex enough to need a team built around it, so that is where we start. For a serious build we assemble a team picked for your particular problem, rather than staffing it from whoever is idle that week. Because the team is chosen and not shuffled in, momentum rarely waits on hiring - a senior engineer often starts immediately, and the team grows as the project itself grows.

Smaller, well-bounded jobs and advisory work take a lighter shape. What we will not do is fit a heavy engagement to a light problem. We would rather match the shape of the work to the shape of the need, and tell you honestly which one that is.
