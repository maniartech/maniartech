Where we are, and are not, the right firm

A good fit

  • The system carries operational or regulatory consequence, and being approximately right is not good enough.
  • Someone needs to own the architecture and be accountable for it later.
  • The work crosses departments, systems or organizations, and the seams are the hard part.
  • You want the reasoning written down, not just the result delivered.

A poor fit

  • You need bodies on a staffing plan and the decisions are already made elsewhere.
  • The lowest bid wins, and depth is not being priced.
  • The requirement is a template site, a plugin configuration, or a one-off script.
  • The timeline is fixed before the problem is understood - we would rather say so than agree to it.

Start with the problem, not a price

Tell us what the system has to be right about. A senior engineer reads it and responds with a technical read - what we would need to understand, what looks risky, and whether we are the right firm at all. An estimate is something we can produce once the problem is understood, not before.
