---
heading: "How we handle your code and your data"
---

- **NDAs come first.** If you want one signed before we discuss anything specific, that is normal here - not friction. Send yours, or ask for ours.
- **Mainstream technology, on purpose.** We build with widely used, hireable tools - Go, Python, React, and PostgreSQL or MongoDB are typical - so the team that maintains the system later needs no special knowledge of ours.
- **Technology decisions are written down.** Stack choices, dependencies, and architecture calls are documented and agreed with you as the project runs, so nothing in your system arrives unannounced.
- **Your project stays off our marketing.** Names, screenshots, and data from your engagement appear on this site only with your consent; our case studies are published with exactly that consent, or anonymized.
