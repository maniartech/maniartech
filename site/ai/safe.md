---
heading: "Why AI with us is safe"
---

AI raises real worries. Here is how we handle each one.

- **A human stays in the loop.** We use AI to assist people, not to replace their judgment. The AI does the heavy lifting and asks a person when it is unsure. We do not ship decisions no one checked.
- **It works from your facts, not made-up ones.** Where AI answers questions, we ground it in your own documents so it points to real sources instead of confidently inventing them.
- **Your data stays yours.** Your documents, data, and prompts are not used to train any third-party model. We are explicit, up front, about what the AI can see, where that data goes, and who processes it.
- **No lock-in.** We build on mainstream technology your own team - or any team - can hire for and maintain. We do not trap you in AI tooling only we understand.
- **Right-sized, not AI for its own sake.** We add AI where it earns its place, and we will not turn a simple need into an expensive experiment to look impressive.
