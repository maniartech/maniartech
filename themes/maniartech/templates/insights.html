{% comment %} No publishing cadence is stated here, deliberately. A frequency is a promise about the future that cannot be verified when it is made and is plainly visible when it breaks - Governing Rule #1 cuts against it. What replaces it is a standard (which we control) and an invitation to falsify us (the strongest thing we can offer a stranger). {% endcomment %}

Nothing goes up here to fill a schedule — there isn't one. If a claim in any piece doesn't hold up, tell us and we'll correct it in the post.
