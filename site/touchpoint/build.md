## What we built

To be precise about the role, because precision is the point: this is not a case of "a client hired ManiarTech to build a product." It is six years of engineering inside one, in two phases.

**Phase one - senior developer, 2012-2017.** Aamir joined Touchpoint Dashboard's engineering team and worked on the platform's core: designing and building many of its modules - the journey-mapping experience, the analytics, the collaboration features - and the continuous work of making the product feel better to use. By his own account he carried a large share of the system's engineering over those years, on a stack that spanned Django, Backbone.js and CoffeeScript through to React and MySQL. He built on infrastructure an earlier engineer had stood up, and made it his own from there.

**Phase two - offshore engineering partner, April 2017 to July 2018.** When Touchpoint Dashboard was acquired by Strativity Inc., the relationship deepened rather than ending. ManiarTech hired two more software engineers, trained them on the product, and became its offshore engineering development partner - a small dedicated team, led by Aamir, carrying the platform's engineering forward under its new owner until the contract concluded in mid-2018.

The product itself was a pioneer of customer-journey management: brands mapped their customers' journeys in it, managed every touchpoint, and collaborated around them. Many Fortune 500 teams used it - a fact we cite as the product's achievement, which we helped engineer, not as our own customer list.
