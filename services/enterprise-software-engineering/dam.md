---
heading: "Enterprise digital asset management"
---

Every company above a certain size has the same quiet problem: the logo lives on a designer's laptop, the current price list is "the one Priya emailed in March", and three versions of the brochure are in circulation - one of them wrong. A digital asset management system is the cure, and it is more than a shared drive: **one governed home** for documents, media and brand assets, where every asset carries its metadata (owner, usage rights, campaign, expiry), versions are kept and the *current* one is unambiguous, roles decide who may view, use or replace an asset, renditions (web, print, thumbnail) are generated rather than hand-exported, and an audit trail records who took what, when.

We build DAM per engagement on a framework we own and actively develop - our own asset platform runs on it, and we have already modernized that platform module by module onto a current Python and PostgreSQL stack, which is the kind of dogfooding we apply before anything reaches a client. Shaped to your taxonomy and your approval rules rather than a one-size subscription: brand governance for marketing, controlled documents for operations, or both on one spine - integrated with the workflow and portal layers above, so an approved asset is also the only one your portal can serve.
