## The situation

Touchpoint Dashboard set out to do something that, in 2012, very few products did well: let a brand map and visualise its customers' journeys, manage every touchpoint along the way, and bring stakeholders together around them. It was built for organisations serious about putting the customer first - and it was aiming at an audience that would hold it to a high standard.

A product like that lives or dies on sustained engineering. Journey mapping, analytics, and multi-stakeholder collaboration are not features you bolt on and walk away from; they need people who stay with the codebase for years, through growth, redesigns, and - as it turned out - an acquisition.

That is the team our founder, Aamir Maniar, joined in 2012 as a senior developer, via UpWork, from ManiarTech - our first international engagement. An earlier engineer had stood up the original infrastructure; Aamir came into a living product and a working team. What began as one developer on the team grew, over six years, into ManiarTech serving as the product's offshore engineering partner.
