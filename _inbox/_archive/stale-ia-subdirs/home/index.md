## ManiarTech Labs: the research firm behind the engineering firm

Anyone can list frameworks on a website. We'd rather show you what we've
built when nobody was paying us to:

- **[Internet Object](/labs/internet-object/)** — we designed a
  schema-first data serialization format from first principles: **50%
  smaller payloads, ~30% fewer LLM tokens than JSON**.
- **[Taj Mahal SSG](/labs/taj-mahal-ssg/)** — a modular static site
  generator, engineered in Go. **This very website runs on it.**
- **[Signals](/labs/signals/)** — our open-source Go pub-sub library,
  **325+ stars** on GitHub.
- **[Web Doodling](/labs/web-doodling/)** — a creative framework for
  HTML5-canvas animations and applications, heading to open source.

This is the difference between a firm that *delivers projects* and a
firm that *advances the craft*. Your project gets the second one.
