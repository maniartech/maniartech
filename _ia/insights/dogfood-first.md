## Outline

- The model: we run our own libraries, frameworks, and standards in production before we
  ever recommend them — "we use this ourselves" is the only endorsement we trust.
- Why so much is deliberately pre-1.0: maturing in the open, on real work, beats a rushed
  version number.
- The trade-off honestly: slower to a public release, but battle-tested before it reaches
  a client.
