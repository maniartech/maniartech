---
heading: "How we work: the ManiarTech Keystone Method"
stages:
  - name: "Survey"
    what: "We learn how the work really flows - the roles, the day-to-day, the pain points, the culture - not the org-chart version."
    gives: "A map of the department as it actually works."
  - name: "Document"
    what: "We capture the processes, formats, and records, so the knowledge lives in the system instead of one person's head."
    gives: "Documented processes and records you own."
  - name: "Design"
    what: "We recommend where automation and integration genuinely help - and, honestly, where they do not."
    gives: "A system and automation design, with the honest calls made."
  - name: "Set the Keystone"
    what: "We build and integrate the system so the structure stands on its own, without depending on any single person."
    gives: "The built, integrated system - live and self-supporting."
  - name: "Bear Load"
    what: "We measure, refine, and move to the next department, carrying the method forward."
    gives: "A metrics view and a steady cadence of improvement."
---

We do not hand you a palace in the sky and wish you luck. We work the way your business actually runs - department by department - in clear, safe stages. Each stage produces something real, and nothing big is committed before the stage before it has proven out. The method aims at a ladder of results - better processes, fewer errors, work that does not stall on one person, and the numbers moving toward where you are actually headed - as the goal of a method, never a guaranteed outcome.
