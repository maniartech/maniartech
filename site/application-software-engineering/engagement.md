---
heading: "How we engage"
---

For complex projects - our primary model - we build a dedicated team around your work: one or more engineers chosen specifically for it, not whoever happens to be free. Work often starts right away with a senior engineer while the rest of the team takes shape, so momentum is never waiting on headcount.

For simpler projects and advisory work, the engagement is lighter: a fixed scope, or time and materials - whatever honestly fits the job. What we do not do is force a big engagement on a small problem.

The details - process, quality gates, and how a project actually runs here - are on [How we work](/how-we-work/).
