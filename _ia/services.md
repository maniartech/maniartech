## What this page does (and deliberately doesn't)
- **Leads with the customer's question** ("what do you need built?"), not "we make technology."
- **Routes, doesn't pitch:** three plain category doors + two cross-cutting capabilities as "brought
  to every engagement" — exactly the structure approved by Aamir (2026-06) per the sitemap.
- **Client proof leads the proof slots; own products are subordinate.** The Enterprise and
  Application cards carry (gated) anonymized client-outcome lines ABOVE the own-products credential —
  closing the review's #1 blocker. Own products are framed as "we run these ourselves," with
  **honest per-product status labels** (Processious in production; Ordin/Tallery in development) — no
  blanket "in production."
- **Real credibility anchor, plainly stated:** a single linked line (27 years; founder ex-JP Morgan
  → About) gives the skeptic a bridge without a flex.

  concrete by naming the mainstream stack (Go/Python/React/PostgreSQL), and "honest estimate"
  replaced by "the estimate shows its assumptions" so the behaviour is the proof.
- **Safety threaded lightly** without turning a routing page into a wall of de-risking — the heavy
  de-risking lives on Home §4 and About §3.
- **Depth/standards/"we make it" deliberately OMITTED** — this is a client routing page; the
  maker-story belongs on Labs / Standards, not the front of Services.
