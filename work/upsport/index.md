## The situation

This one did not start with a cold call. Jeff Hines had spent years watching us engineer his previous company's platform, Touchpoint Dashboard - and when he started his next venture, UpSport (IntelMaven LLC), he came back to ManiarTech. A founder choosing the same engineering team a second time is about the clearest vote of confidence we get.

His idea was a hard one. UpSport began as a recruitment platform - helping players of all levels get seen by coaches, agents and recruiters - and grew toward something more ambitious: letting coaches review player video and actually coach through it. Not just watch footage, but mark it up, talk over it, and hand a player something they could learn from.

Jeff wanted all of it to live online, in the browser, so a coach could open a player's match video anywhere and go straight to work. And that was the open question: in 2019, recording commentary and drawing on a running video, live, entirely inside a browser sat at the edge of what the technology could do. The risk was never the design. It was whether the browser could carry it.
