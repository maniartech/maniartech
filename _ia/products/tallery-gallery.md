## 3 · WHAT TALLERY GALLERY DOES  *(the capability, plainly — and shown, not just told)*

### One home for your team's digital and media assets.

Tallery Gallery is a **collaborative digital and media asset management framework** — the engine
behind our Enterprise DAM service. It gives a team one place to store, organize, share, discuss, and
govern its media:

- **Organize** — repositories, folders, and tags, with fast search and trash/restore, so assets are
  easy to find and hard to lose.
- **Collaborate** — share via controlled links or named collaborators; comment and discuss in
  threads attached to the asset itself; see a full activity history.
- **Control access** — multi-account/organization structure with roles and invitations, so people
  see exactly what they should and nothing more.
- **Keep a record** — activity logging of key actions, plus GDPR-aware data handling
  (anonymize/export). `[verify which compliance features are client-facing vs. internal at launch.]`
