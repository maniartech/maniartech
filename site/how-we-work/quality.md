---
heading: "What we hold ourselves to, as behaviors"
---

We do not keep our standards on a poster. They show up as things we do, and things you can check:

- **We review before you do.** Before anything reaches you, we go over it ourselves - the code, the design, and whether it genuinely fits the people who will use it. As a rule most of our work clears the first client review with only minor changes; a very particular client is the exception, not the norm.
- **We fix causes, not symptoms.** When we repair a defect we go for its root, so one fix does not spawn ten more. Over the life of a project the count of open defects tends to fall rather than churn - no whack-a-mole.
- **We build things people actually use.** Performance and fitness for the real job are part of what we mean by done. A system that passes a demo and then gets quietly worked around is not finished, however clever it looks.
- **We staff the work with experienced engineers.** Your project is not where anyone learns the basics. Experience wastes less of both our time and yours.
