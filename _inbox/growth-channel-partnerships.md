## The core fit (why this strategy suits Aamir specifically)
Aamir's weakness is selling/self-promotion (he's a maker, not a marketer — see the "core
problem" note). This strategy **routes around that weakness**: partner with people whose strength
IS sales/client relationships, and let them sell while ManiarTech builds. The human version of
"the work markets itself." On-strategy and authentic to who he is.

## Three growth channels (keep distinct)
1. **Channel / delivery partnerships (the main new idea):** connect with **Business Developers
   on LinkedIn + agencies/companies that already HAVE clients** but need technology delivery →
   ManiarTech becomes their **technology (delivery) partner.**
2. **Campaigns** — outbound/paid pushes (later).
3. **Organic inbound** — companies approaching ManiarTech after seeing **Indigo, Internet
   Object,** etc. go public. *(Real, but see caveat below.)*

> ⚠️ **Distinct from the "Technology Partnership" SERVICE** (`service-technology-partnership.md`),
> which is a **co-founder / equity / shared-venture** model (Love-and-Beyond style). THIS is a
> **delivery/reseller** model: partner brings a client, ManiarTech builds, paid per project.
> Different partners, different deals — don't conflate.

## Business model — two options Aamir is weighing (honest tradeoffs)
| | **A) Fixed price, partner marks up** (wholesale) | **B) ManiarTech quotes, partner gets commission** (referral) |
|---|---|---|
| ManiarTech gets | Predictable (its number) | Its number + keeps the client relationship |
| Partner motivation | High (owns the margin) | Lower (finder's fee) |
| **The catch** | Usually becomes **white-label → ManiarTech stays INVISIBLE** | Partner may resent ManiarTech being visible to "their" client |

**★ The key tension (the most important strategic point):** the markup/white-label model
**perpetuates invisibility — the exact problem this whole project exists to fix.** A hidden
subcontractor earns revenue but stays unknown, with no independent pipeline.
**Recommendation:** early on, **prefer transparent / co-branded partnerships** ("delivered by
ManiarTech") so each deal earns BOTH revenue AND visibility. Take pure white-label only when the
revenue clearly justifies the anonymity (or once an independent brand exists). The **price
estimator is a ready partner-enablement tool** (partners quote with it; "Estimated by ManiarTech"
keeps visibility even inside their deals).

## On the OSS-inbound channel (honest calibration)
"Companies will approach me when Indigo/IO go public" — TRUE, and the best kind of growth (warm
inbound from proven capability). **But publishing ≠ being seen.** "It's on GitHub" won't bring
companies; a **launch moment** will (Show HN, r/golang, the benchmark people share). So this
channel is real but **downstream of actually launching + promoting** the projects — loops back to
the distribution muscle. Don't bank on "build it and they'll come" without the launch.

## Ethics screen (Aamir 2026-06) — applies to ALL inbound, esp. partner deals
ManiarTech **declines ethically/legally questionable work upfront** (always has). In the channel
model this is **brand protection** — others bring the deals, ManiarTech's name goes on them. Make
"the work we'll take / won't take" an explicit **partnership criterion.** [[values-how-we-work]]

## Sequencing (Aamir's instinct = correct; matches DISTRIBUTION-STRATEGY)
**Converting website + working estimator FIRST, then channels.** Not for tidiness — because
EVERY channel (BD partners, campaigns, OSS inbound) points people back at the site/estimator. If
those don't convert, the channel is wasted. The site is the **foundation all three stand on.**
Then test ONE channel first (likely warm BD/agency partners — relationship-based, needs no
marketing from Aamir), measure, adjust. The map fills in as he walks it.

## ★ Delivery capacity — the team/pool problem (Aamir 2026-06: "we need to solve this")
**Principle (non-negotiable):** experienced people ONLY — **no freshers / just-passed-out grads /
inexperienced consultants.** Aamir lost big money/time to them; won't repeat it. (= the
"senior-only delivery" value, [[values-how-we-work]].) The costly lesson bought him the JUDGMENT
to vet — that's an asset.

**⚠️ The bar is EXPERIENCED *AND* CURRENT** (Aamir corrected 2026-06: "friend circle pool is
outdated"). Years alone don't qualify — need **senior + still hands-on with the MODERN stack**
(Go, modern AI, modern web). His old 27-yr network/friend circle is experienced but NOT current →
**NOT the primary seed** (individual still-current ex-colleagues aside). This is a scarcer profile
→ smaller, more deliberately built pool; quality over quantity (on-ethos).

**The trap:** chicken-and-egg — need experienced people to win work; can't bench/pay them before
work exists. **Solution shape = vetted, ON-CALL associate network, NOT payroll:**
- **Pre-vet now, activate later.** Curate a pool of experienced-AND-current freelance/contract
  engineers + specialists, personally vetted; they stay on their own work; activate per project.
  Pay only when there's revenue. The work NOW (while quiet) = **vetting + relationships** (time,
  not salary) — doable in parallel with the site.
- **★ BEST SEED = his own published projects as a RECRUITING MAGNET.** People who engage with
  **Indigo / Internet Object / signals / UExL** (contributors, issue-filers, real users)
  self-select on all three needs at once: **current + skilled + aligned with his standards/style**
  (their code/questions reveal their level — no guessing). → Publishing/launching the projects
  feeds TWO pipelines: clients AND talent. Another reason launches matter.
- **Other current sources:** referrals from CURRENT practitioners ("best you've worked with
  recently," not 15 yrs ago); vetted senior-freelance platforms; current project collaborators
  (e.g. Tallery's 2nd engineer).
- **Repeatable vetting bar tests CURRENCY directly:** a small **paid trial on a real modern-stack
  task** + real technical conversation + trusted reference, before client work. (Not a résumé.)
- **Tier it:** a couple of trusted **senior leads** (client-facing / engagement leads) + a wider
  pool of experienced specialists activated per project. Grow a salaried CORE only as **recurring**
  revenue supports it — never ahead of it.
- **Tooling = fewer people needed:** Booster + AI-assisted dev + own frameworks multiply each
  engineer → the team he needs is SMALLER than a normal shop's (also underpins ~50% cost).
- ⚠️ **Honest caveats:** a bench only works PAIRED with deal flow (idle pools go stale — people
  take other commitments); don't over-build before the work is real; start with a handful, grow
  with demand; it's ongoing relationship work, not a one-time fix.
- **Website framing:** "senior, experienced engineers only — we don't learn on your project" as a
  STANDARD; never claim a big employed team; pair with "scaling deliberately." Must stay true.

## ★ Sharpening on the pool problem (Claude, 2026-06 — recommendation, validate with Aamir)
- **Can't solve the pool BEFORE the pipeline.** A pre-built standing bench *decays* — experienced-
  and-current people won't sit idle; they take other commitments and drift. So a pool without deal
  flow doesn't sit there, it **evaporates.** → Sequence: pipeline first (converting site / profile /
  demos / estimator + BD partner bringing deals) → grow capacity **just-in-time, project by project.**
  Don't pre-build a bench. (Corollary: building the profile + demos IS step 1 of solving the team.)
- **★ The real unlock is 1–2 trusted senior LEADS who can own an engagement end-to-end WITHOUT
  Aamir — not a big pool.** This breaks Aamir-as-bottleneck, genuinely answers the bus-factor, lets
  bigger work be taken, AND lets the wider pool be managed by someone other than Aamir. Without a
  lead, a bigger pool just = **more people for Aamir to supervise** (more supervision cost — the exact
  thing the Keystone Method kills). The senior lead is the keystone of ManiarTech's own talent plan.
- **Honest constraint:** senior-only + small pool = **deliberate, steady growth, NOT explosive scale.**
  Size deals to real fieldable capacity; use PoC-first + phased delivery (already practised — Sales
  Navigator's "70-80% base, co-develop the rest") to take work without a standing army.
- **Near-term realistic seed** (vs the medium-term OSS magnet): the people already active (son,
  Nomaan), Tallery's engineer, referrals from *current* practitioners, 1–2 seniors vetted via a real
  paid micro-task — kept warm with actual (even small) paid work, not a bench.
