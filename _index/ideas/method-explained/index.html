Style proof | one section

How a department stops depending on one person

The same idea as the arch and the graph, told the plain way: five steps, one diagram, one change at a time. Scroll.

One change per stepEach step moves exactly one thing in the diagram, so the motion is the explanation.
Real words, real rolesSales, Orders, Billing. Plain labels, no decoration.
Colour means "this changed"Mint appears only on what the step just did. No glow, no particles, no camera moves.
01 | Survey

See who everything depends on.

We start with the people and the handoffs: who knows how the work gets done, and who has to ask them.

02 | Document

Get it out of one head.

Processes, formats and records are written down, so the knowledge lives on pages and not in a person.

03 | Design

Point the work at the page.

Each handoff is redesigned to go to the written process. We also say plainly where a system will not help.

04 | Set the keystone

Make the system the centre.

The documented system takes the middle. The person reviews it. The work no longer waits for them.

05 | Bear load

Then let it carry the work.

Motivation, skill and results are measured, and the method moves on to the next department.
