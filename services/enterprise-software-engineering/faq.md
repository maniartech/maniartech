---
heading: "Questions buyers actually ask"
faqs:
  - q: "What is enterprise software engineering?"
    a: "It is the software your operations run on - process automation and integration, digital asset management, and analytics - built as systems your business depends on day to day rather than one-off tools. We design, build, and integrate them, department by department."
  - q: "What is business process automation and integration?"
    a: "It means automating the manual, repetitive work - approvals, hand-offs, escalations, audit trails - and connecting the systems that do not talk to each other, so data flows between your tools instead of being re-keyed by hand."
  - q: "Do you build custom systems or sell a product?"
    a: "We build systems tailored to how you actually work. Where it genuinely helps, we deliver on our own process-automation platform, Processious, which is in production for a client today - but the system is yours, built to fit your operation."
  - q: "Who owns the code, and are we locked in?"
    a: "You own the code and the IP. We build on mainstream technology - Go, Python, React, PostgreSQL - that any competent team can maintain, and our own platform enters only where it clearly helps you, never to trap you."
  - q: "Can you automate one department at a time?"
    a: "Yes, and that is how we prefer to work. The ManiarTech Keystone Method runs department by department in staged steps, so nothing big is committed before the step before it has proven out. You are never forced into a single big-bang rollout."
  - q: "Can you handle our digital asset management?"
    a: "Yes. We build a governed home for your documents, media, and brand assets, with role-based access, versioning, and an audit trail. We build this per engagement on a framework we own and are actively developing, not as an off-the-shelf product you sign up for."
  - q: "Are you certified, and can we verify it?"
    a: "Yes. We are certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 by URS under UKAS accreditation, certificates 123961/B/0001 and 123961/A/0001. The registrar will confirm them directly if you email info at urs-certification.com."
