ManiarTech is a small, senior team that builds the layers beneath software — production systems for clients, plus the standards we've authored, our libraries, and a programming language in development. If you're an experienced engineer who cares more about doing the work *right* than doing it fast, we should talk.

## Who we're looking for

- **Experienced and current.** Senior engineers who are still hands-on with modern stacks — not résumé-deep but practice-shallow. We don't hire juniors to learn on client work.
- **Craftspeople.** People who care about correctness, clarity, and the parts most teams skip.
- **Honest.** You'd rather tell a client the truth than win the argument.
- **Deep over fast.** You're drawn to hard problems and willing to sit with them until they're right.

> If you've built frameworks, contributed to serious open source, or quietly obsess over getting the foundations correct — you'll fit here.

## What you'd work on

- **Real client systems** — process automation, custom platforms, modernizations — built properly, on mainstream technology (Go, Python, React, PostgreSQL).
- **Our own technology** — the open-source ecosystem, the standards we've authored, and **Indigo**, our Go superset language in development. The foundational work most software is built on top of, you'd help build.
- Both, usually. We're a lab and a delivery team at once.

## How we work

- **Senior-led, no churn.** Small team, high trust, no body-shop treadmill.
- **Honest engineering.** Reproducible benchmarks, honest status labels, "no output is better than wrong output." We don't ship what we can't stand behind.
- **We dogfood.** We run our own work on the tools we build before anyone else does.
- **Patient.** We ship when it's right. Ideas are carried for years until the research yields a real answer. This is genuinely how we build — come if that excites you, not if it frustrates you.

## How we engage

You'd work with a senior core led by **Aamir Maniar (Managing Director & Engineering Head)**, with **Oomera Maniar** on operations and a vetted network of specialist engineers brought in by discipline. We're growing deliberately: today we work with senior engineers on a project and associate basis, and we grow our core team as the work justifies it. So the door is open in a few ways:

- **Associate / contract** — experienced engineers we bring onto specific engagements.
- **Core team** — as we grow.
- **Open source** — the best way to get our attention: engage with our public work — issues, pull requests, ideas. We notice the people who get it.

**Our bar, so you know what to expect:** a real technical conversation, and a small paid trial on real work — because we hire on what you *do*, not what your CV says.

We're not advertising a specific open role right now, but we're always glad to hear from engineers who'd fit. If that's you, say hello — tell us about yourself and the work you care about. It's also worth reading a little more [about how we work](/about/).
