## The calls that mattered

**Scope it to presales - deliberately.** Bookings, payments, post-sales, construction tracking: all of it was tempting, and all of it stayed out of version one. We mapped the developer's actual presales workflow and built exactly that, on a foundation designed to extend into fuller sales management later. A smaller system the team actually runs beats a sprawling one they work around.

**Make the estimate a governed workflow, not a calculator.** Taxes and charges are configurable masters, not hard-coded numbers - GST, stamp duty, registration, jurisdiction by jurisdiction. Discounts go through an approval workflow rather than a salesperson's discretion. The output is a branded, RERA-referenced estimate sheet. The point is that every number a customer sees is computed, governed, and reproducible - not assembled by hand at a desk.

**Hide the inventory from customers.** Unit availability - available, on hold, sold - is live for sales staff and invisible to customers. Negotiating position is part of the business; the platform respects it by design instead of leaking it.

**Put content in the client's hands.** Projects, slides, images, brochures, floor plans, price lists - all managed through the admin panel and asset library. The developer updates their own showcase without calling us. We would rather be needed for engineering than for edits.
