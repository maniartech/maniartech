## Abstract

Before you commit budget to a custom build, it helps to understand what actually drives its cost. This paper is a practical guide to reading an estimate before you sign one — the factors that move the price most, and the ones teams consistently under- or over-estimate.

## What it will cover

- What really moves the price: scope clarity, integration surface, data migration, compliance and security depth, and unknowns.
- What teams routinely misjudge, in both directions.
- How to read an estimate critically before committing budget.
- Ranges over false precision — and how this maps to the Estimator's actual inputs.
