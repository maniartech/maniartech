## What it is

Enterprise DAM = a governed, searchable home for an organization's **digital and media
assets** — images, video, brand collateral, product media, documents — with the access
control, collaboration, versioning, and compliance a real enterprise needs. ManiarTech
delivers this as a **built solution on the Tallery Gallery framework**, tailored to the
client (not a one-size SaaS sign-up).

The problem it solves: enterprises accumulate assets across scattered drives, inboxes, and
tools — no single source of truth, no governance, duplicated work, brand inconsistency, and
asset knowledge locked in individuals. A DAM makes assets **findable, governed, reusable,
and safe to collaborate on.**

## What it covers (capabilities — real, from the Tallery Gallery framework)

These map directly to Tallery Gallery's actual modules (so the capability is provable, not
aspirational):

- **Asset organization** — repositories, folders, tags, search; trash/restore. *(assets module)*
- **Collaboration** — shareable links (view-only / password / expiry), collaborators,
  threaded comments, activity logs. *(collaboration module)*
- **Access & governance** — multi-account/organization, **role-based access control**,
  invitations, document/asset locking. *(membership module)*
- **Compliance** — audit trails, **GDPR** anonymize/export. *(compliance module)*
- **Storage & scale** — S3-backed uploads; modern stack (Django/PostgreSQL). *(infra)*
- **Versioning & lifecycle** — asset versions, controlled lifecycle.

> All of the above are **already built or in active development in Tallery Gallery's V2**
> (server ~70–80% per Aamir). The service delivers these as a tailored enterprise solution.

## Fit with the portfolio & the Keystone Method

- **DAM is usually a department/function** (marketing, brand, creative, product, legal). So
  an Enterprise DAM engagement fits the **Keystone Method** cleanly: Survey the asset
  function → Document its assets/processes → Design the DAM → Set the Keystone (the built
  system as the asset system-of-record) → Bear Load (adoption, governance, metrics).
  [[service-business-process-automation]]
- **Ties to the "document everything / reduce bus-factor" thesis:** a DAM is literally how an
  org stops asset knowledge from living in one person's drive — same resilience story.
- **Cross-sell with BPA:** asset-heavy processes (marketing ops, product launches, brand
  governance) often need both process automation *and* a DAM. Natural bundle.
