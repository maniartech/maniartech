---
heading: "How we work: the ManiarTech Keystone Method"
stages:
  - name: "Survey"
    what: "We sit with the people who do the work and map how it really flows - roles, hand-offs, exceptions, workarounds - not the org-chart version. The workarounds are usually where the truth lives."
    gives: "A process inventory and role map of the department as it actually works."
  - name: "Document"
    what: "We write down the processes, record formats, and rules - who may do what, in which order, and what happens on rejection - so the knowledge stops living in one person's head."
    gives: "Documented workflows, a role/permission matrix, and record definitions in writing."
  - name: "Design"
    what: "We design the system around the states the work moves through, and make the honest calls: where automation genuinely helps, where a plain form is enough, and what should not be built at all."
    gives: "A system design with the workflow model, integration points, and the no-build calls written down."
  - name: "Set the Keystone"
    what: "We build and integrate the system and take a department live on it - real records, real users, real cutover plan - so the structure stands without depending on any single person."
    gives: "The built, integrated system - live, with training and a documented cutover behind it."
  - name: "Bear Load"
    what: "We watch the metrics the system now produces, fix at root cause, and carry the method to the next department with everything the last one taught us."
    gives: "A live metrics view, a punch list burning down, and the next department scoped."
---

We work the way your business actually runs - department by department, in stages that each end in something real you can inspect. Nothing big is committed before the stage before it has proven out, and each stage's deliverable is named up front, so "how is the project going" always has a concrete answer.
