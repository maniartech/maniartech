## How it turned out

The lab moved from a 600-form maze to a single workflow screen - far faster and far easier to learn and use than the Access original, with the whole lab plus its accounting in one role-governed application. The engagement itself is a signal: the lab came back to extend the system to the web, a customer report-download portal, and kept us engaged for around two years in total.

Then there is the long tail. By the lab's account - we hear it from people who worked there - the application is still in production today, around fifteen years on, with some of their own later modifications. We are no longer in contact with the lab and do not maintain the system, so we state that as the lab reports it rather than as a firm claim of our own.

Still: fifteen years of daily production, through real workflows and real load, is the best test of how software is built that we know of. The system went live around 2011, and the lab has run it on its own for well over a decade since. We consider both halves of that sentence a result.
