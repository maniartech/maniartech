## What changed vs. v2
- **Bus-factor fix (HIGH):** every singular "a senior engineer" reassurance reworked to team-continuity
  language — "people who can actually answer," "one of our senior engineers," "a senior engineer on
  our team," plus an explicit continuity line ("someone always responds, and you're never waiting on a
  single person"). The conversion page no longer deepens the "is it just one guy?" fear.
- **ISO line gated (HIGH):** the bald "ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 certified" line is now a gated template
  requiring real cert#s + accreditation body + a verify link, with an explicit instruction to CUT it
  if those aren't ready — not ship it unverifiable.
- **Client proof + JP Morgan bridge added (MED):** new §2 puts one light verifiable proof point and
  the JP Morgan pedigree (with a required LinkedIn link) at the decision point — both placeholdered,
  never fabricated.
- **Estimator copy softened (MED):** no longer promises the buyer "will see we understand your
  problem"; describes what the guided estimate does. Stopped leading with "Our AI" (jargon DON'T list).
- **Primary CTA differentiated (LOW):** "Get a free project estimate" is now the visually primary
  button; email + human are secondary, on the page and in the closing CTA.
- **SMB-welcome added (LOW):** "whether you're a small team or an enterprise" in the hero and close.
- **Privacy reassurance added:** "we won't add you to a mailing list or hand your details to anyone."
- **Launch-gate checklist added to §5 (LOW)** with an owner, so the page can't ship half-filled.
- **Placeholders left, all needing Aamir:** founder years + LinkedIn URL · one true proof point ·
  team-size wording ("senior team" vs "small senior team") · public email address · "talk to a human"
  destination · honest response-time window · legal entity name · registered address · jurisdiction
  (+ reg. number) · optional phone · ISO certificate numbers / accreditation body / verify link.
