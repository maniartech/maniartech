You probably found us because you need a custom application built properly the first time, by people who've done it before. Maybe you have an idea or a product and need a team that can take it from discovery to shipped. Maybe you've outgrown your tools — spreadsheets, manual work, or off-the-shelf software that no longer fits. Or maybe you have a system that already exists and want an honest expert to review it and tell you what to fix, and what to leave alone. And maybe you've been burned before, by a vendor who over-promised, staffed juniors, or left you locked into tools only they understood.

We're built for exactly these. We design, build, and ship custom software — web apps, line-of-business systems, SaaS products, internal tools, integrations — and we review and improve the systems you already have. Senior engineers only, on a stack your team can hire for, delivered on an ISO-certified process.

## Two ways we work with you

**Build — any application, end to end.** Web apps, line-of-business systems, SaaS products, internal tools, integrations — full lifecycle, from discovery and architecture through build, ship, and support. We choose the right tool for the job from a proven, mainstream stack — Go, Python, React, PostgreSQL — never an exotic one you can't staff for later.

**Advise — review, audit, improve.** Already have a system, and not sure it's built right? We review the architecture, audit for performance and security, and give you a straight recommendation: what to fix, what to modernize, what to leave alone, and whether to build or buy. It's the difference between hiring coders and hiring an engineering partner.

## Why it's safe to build with us

- **Experienced engineers do your work.** We don't learn on your project. The work is done by a senior core plus a vetted network of specialist engineers, so your project isn't riding on one person's availability.
- **No lock-in.** We build on mainstream technology your own team — or any team — can hire for: Go, Python, React, PostgreSQL. We never trap you in tools only we understand.
- **Right-sized, never over-engineered.** We build to solve your problem at the right scale, not a science experiment. The advisory side cuts both ways — we'll tell you what *not* to build, too.
- **Predictable by process.** ISO 9001:2015 (quality) and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (security) certified by URS under UKAS accreditation (cert. 123961/B/0001 and 123961/A/0001; verify at info@urs-certification.com) — so you can check the certificate rather than take our word.
- **Straight talk.** We'll tell you the truth — including when something isn't the right fit, or shouldn't be built at all.
