## How it turned out

The platform is live at sales.shanteehomes.com - you can open it yourself. From start to launch took about four months, for the full scope: showcase, presentation builder, projects portfolio, interactive floor plans, the estimation-and-payment-plan engine, the asset library, and role-based access control.

That is all we will claim for now. The system is newly launched, and we do not report business outcomes it has not had time to produce - no invented conversion figures, no projected sales impact. When it has been in the field long enough for real numbers to exist, we will add them here.

What the build itself demonstrates is breadth at speed: a genuinely comprehensive platform, engineered end to end by one team in a matter of months. That pace is possible because we compose new systems from building blocks we have already proven elsewhere rather than starting every project from scratch.
