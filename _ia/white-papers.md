*(Launch state — show only when ≥1 paper is live:)*

A good white paper should make a decision easier, not sell you something. So ours follow a few rules:

- **A real point of view** — every paper starts from a position we've actually earned in the work,
  not a regurgitated listicle. If we don't have something worth saying, we don't publish.
- **Evidence over assertion** — we cite data, research, and real examples, and we're honest about
  where the evidence runs out.
- **Useful for a decision you're actually making** — costing a project, choosing what to automate,
  planning a modernization.
- **Open by default** — most are free to read in full, no email required.
