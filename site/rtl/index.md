## The situation

Reliable Analytical Laboratories is one of Mumbai's prominent analytical testing labs. It runs thousands of tests, grouped in series, each with many parameters to check against requirements, through a genuinely complex pipeline: a sample is received, logged, accepted by the lab, analysed, reviewed, signed off more than once, then dispatched and invoiced.

The lab's own team had built its record-tracking system in-house, in Microsoft Access. Over years of growth it had become substantial - more than 600 forms, about as many reports, and a sprawl of data tables wrapped around the lab's real workflows. It captured a great deal, and it reflected a deep understanding of the work. But it had outgrown the tool it was built in. It was slow under its own weight, and hard to learn: every new member of staff had to be trained across that maze of forms before they could do their job.

This was not a case of replacing a failed vendor system. It was taking a deeply understood internal system - one the lab's own people knew end to end - and making it fast, learnable, and built to last. We worked hand in hand with the team that knew every workflow in it.
