The hardest things we have built, we built for ourselves: libraries, frameworks, data and language tooling, and platforms - the foundational layer most application software merely sits on top of. A team that has written a parser, a storage engine seam, or an expression VM does not get intimidated by a complex business application.

We do not make you adopt any of it - your application is built on mainstream tools you can hire for. But it is why hard requirements are comfortably in range, and you can check the proof yourself:

- [Internet Object](/foundry/internet-object/), our compact data format - 40-60% smaller than minified JSON, with a playground you can run in your browser.
- [UExL](/foundry/uexl/), our expression engine - zero allocations on the hot path, and fastest in the scenarios measured. Run the benchmark yourself.
- [signals](/foundry/signals/), our open-source Go event library, public on GitHub.
- [Processious](/products/processious/), our process-automation platform - the same one running a client's laboratory system in production today.
