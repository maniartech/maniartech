The failure mode of AI-drafted documents is well known: fluent text with no provenance, drawing on the open internet when it should draw on *your* material, and no way for a reviewer to check a single sentence. Documentor.AI is our product answer to that failure mode: **describe the document you want, and it drafts from your own knowledge - keeping the path from every claim back to its source.**

## Known limits

- **Early-stage, in development.** Not a customer deployment, and not presented as one - the same wording our Applied AI page uses for it, kept consistent here.
- **Private.** No public artifact, trial or package exists yet.
- **No performance or quality metrics are published**, because none have been measured to our publishing bar.

## Status: four facts, kept separate

- **Availability** - private.
- **Licence** - proprietary.
- **Maturity** - **early-stage, in development.**
- **Adoption** - not available. A demonstration for qualified customers is possible by arrangement.

## What this demonstrates

Documentor is our Applied AI discipline pointed at ourselves: suitability first, grounding before generation, citations as a structural requirement rather than a feature, and a human in the loop with the evidence in view. Building it teaches us first-hand what that discipline costs to implement - which is exactly the experience an enterprise customer wants in the room when their own document workflows meet a language model.
