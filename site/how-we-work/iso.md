The behaviors above are what these management systems mean in day-to-day practice. The registration is the formal evidence that those behaviors are a documented process rather than good intentions - examined against the standard by an independent, accredited auditor, and re-examined on a recurring cycle rather than awarded once and framed. That cycle is also why we do not ask you to trust a status printed on a website: whether a certificate is in force today is the registrar's answer to give, and we publish the numbers so you can ask them directly.
