## 2 · WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR  *(set the bar honestly)*
- **Experienced and current.** Senior engineers who are still hands-on with modern stacks — not
  résumé-deep but practice-shallow. We don't hire juniors to learn on client work.
- **Craftspeople.** People who care about correctness, clarity, and the parts most teams skip.
- **Honest.** You'd rather tell a client the truth than win the argument.
- **Deep over fast.** You're drawn to hard problems and willing to sit with them until they're right.

> If you've built frameworks, contributed to serious open source, or quietly obsess over getting
> the foundations correct — you'll fit here.

---

## 3 · WHAT YOU'D WORK ON
- **Real client systems** — process automation, custom platforms, modernizations — built properly,
  on mainstream technology (Go, Python, React, PostgreSQL).
- **Our own technology** — the open-source ecosystem, the standards we've authored, and **Indigo**,
  our in-development Go superset language. The foundational work most software is built on top of,
  you'd help build.
- Both, usually. We're a lab and a delivery team at once.

---

## 4 · HOW WE WORK
- **Senior-only, no churn.** Small team, high trust, no body-shop treadmill.
- **Honest engineering.** Reproducible benchmarks, honest status labels, "no output is better than
  wrong output." We don't ship what we can't stand behind.
- **We dogfood.** We run our own work on the tools we build before anyone else does.
- **Patient.** We ship when it's right. Ideas are carried for years until the research yields a real
  answer. *(This is genuinely how we build — come if that excites you, not if it frustrates you.)*

## 5 · HOW WE ENGAGE  *(honest about the model)*
**Who you'd work with:** a senior core led by **Aamir Maniar (Managing Director & Engineering Head)**,
with **Oomera Maniar** on operations and a vetted network of specialist engineers brought in by
discipline. We're **growing deliberately.** Today we work with senior engineers on a **project and
associate basis**, and we grow our core team as the work justifies it. So the door is open in a few
ways:
- **Associate / contract** — experienced engineers we bring onto specific engagements.
- **Core team** — as we grow.
- **Open source** — the best way to get our attention: engage with our public work (issues, PRs,
  ideas). We notice the people who get it.

**Our bar (so you know what to expect):** a real technical conversation, and a small paid trial on
real work — because we hire on what you *do*, not what your CV says.
