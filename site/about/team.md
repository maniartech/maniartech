ManiarTech is led by **Aamir Maniar**, Managing Director and Engineering Head, with **Oomera Maniar** running operations. Client work is **led and reviewed by our senior engineering core**, with a vetted network of specialist engineers engaged where the problem requires them. We do not substitute trainees for the experienced engineers presented during the engagement. We'd rather be small and excellent than large and average, and we add people only when the work and our standard both justify it.

Because we work by documented process — the same discipline behind our independently audited ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 management systems — the work lives in our systems, not in one person's head.
