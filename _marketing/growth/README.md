# ManiarTech Growth Program - the post-launch execution system

> The single operating manual for marketing/SEO/distribution execution, starting the week
> maniartech.com v2 goes live. Designed for a SOLE FOUNDER: time-boxed, checklist-driven,
> AI-assisted (write, do not perform), with scheduled verification and a CAPA loop
> (corrective and preventive action) when a checkpoint misses.
>
> Strategy source: `_inbox/SEO-PLAN.md` + `_inbox/DISTRIBUTION-STRATEGY.md`. This directory is
> the EXECUTION layer - what to do, when, how to verify, and how to correct course.
> Governing Rule #1 applies to marketing too: every public claim true, sourced, calibrated.

## The one-line objective
Turn the v2 site + the authored technology into compounding organic reach: visible effects by
month 3 (leading indicators), full effect by months 6-9 (rankings + steady inbound estimates).

## Honest expectation setting (read once, believe it)
- 3 months = LEADING indicators: pages indexed, impressions trending up, first launch spike(s),
  brand-term ownership, first organic estimate submissions. NOT a flood of leads.
- 6-9 months = COMPOUNDING: Tier 1/2 keyword rankings (see SEO-PLAN tiers), steady referral
  traffic from launches/links, predictable weekly inbound. SEO pays like compound interest -
  slow, then suddenly.
- Nothing here fabricates signals: no paid links, no fake reviews, no engagement pods.

## Weekly time budget (sole founder, sustainable)
- Baseline: **6-8 hours/week** - Mon 1.5h (social batch + schedule), Wed 2-3h (content block),
  Fri 1h (metrics + checklist), + ad-hoc replies.
- Launch weeks: **+4-6 hours** (launch day needs presence for comments). Max ~1 launch/month.
- If a week collapses (client crunch): do ONLY the Mon social batch (45 min). The system
  survives skipped weeks; it does not survive abandoned months.

## How AI is used (the "automation" model)
Aamir does not perform; AI drafts, Aamir approves. Concretely:
1. Weekly batch: open a Claude session in this repo and say
   "Run the weekly social batch per _marketing/growth/SOCIAL-AI-PIPELINE.md" -
   it drafts the week's posts from the dossiers/commits/launch material, Rule-#1-checked.
2. Aamir edits/approves (target: 30 minutes), schedules via LinkedIn native scheduler
   (or Buffer free plan).
3. Same model for blog drafts, launch copy, directory descriptions, HARO pitches:
   AI drafts from the canon, Aamir approves. Nothing publishes without his eyes.
- HARD RULE: no auto-posting without human approval. No automation AT ALL on Reddit/HN
  (communities detect and punish it; launches are done by hand, personally).

## Scope now vs later
IN now: site launch amplification, LinkedIn (personal+company), OSS launches, blog/insights,
directories + GBP + entity, review collection, HARO/podcast outreach, metrics + CAPA.
LATER (add as products mature): newsletter, estimate-library at scale, paid experiments,
partner co-marketing, product-specific funnels (Documentor, Processious), X/Twitter expansion.
