## 3 · HOW WE WORK  *(values as behaviors + de-risk — why you're safe)*

### We put our standards in the work, not on a poster.

- **Experienced engineers do your work** — we don't learn on your project.
- **No lock-in** — we build on mainstream, hireable technology (Go, Python, React, PostgreSQL); you
  (or any team) can maintain it. We never slip our own libraries into your codebase without your
  say-so — you own the code, and you can walk.
- **We check before we hand off** — every deliverable is reviewed for quality, design, and fit for
  your real users *before* it reaches you, so what you get is finished work, not a rough draft.
  `[softened from "clears your review first pass" — overclaim per cold panel.]`
- **Real fixes** — when we fix a defect, it stays fixed and doesn't spawn new ones; bug counts fall
  as a project matures.
- **Predictable by process** — ISO 9001:2015 (a real, audited quality process) and ISO/IEC 27001:2022
  (a real security standard for your data) certified, by **URS** under **UKAS** accreditation
  (cert. 123961/B/0001 & 123961/A/0001 — verify at info@urs-certification.com). Delivery comes
  from process, not heroics. `[ISO wired 2026-06. NO printed expiry — recert audits Jul/Aug 2026,
  certification continuous from June; add validity when the new cert issues.]`
- **We tell you the truth** — including when something isn't the right fit, or shouldn't be built.
